Welcome to Nepal Bhumi

Comfortable rooms in the heart of Kathmandu’s traveller quarter

Hotel Nepal Bhumi is a 3-star hotel on Amrit Marg in Thamel, close to Kathmandu Durbar Square and about 5 km (3.1 miles) from Tribhuvan International Airport.

Every room is air-conditioned with a private bathroom, breakfast is included, and our team is at the front desk around the clock.

  • Free breakfast
  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Free parking
  • Airport shuttle
  • Restaurant & lounge bar
  • Air-conditioned rooms
  • 24-hour front desk
  • Pet-friendly options
